# KPIs & Business Value

This section covers how engineering speed, delivery effectiveness, and code quality are actually measured - not as vanity metrics, but as the numbers that tell us whether the standards elsewhere in this playbook are working.

## What this section covers

| Page | What it answers |
|---|---|
| [Engineering Metrics](/kpis/engineering-metrics) | The DORA metrics used to measure engineering speed and stability, extracted automatically from CI/CD and version control |
| [Delivery Performance](/kpis/delivery-performance) | SPACE Framework metrics for how effectively the team delivers business value |
| [Quality Metrics](/kpis/quality-metrics) | Metrics that track codebase maintainability and the effectiveness of Quality Gates |

## Where this leads

These metrics are collected against the standards defined in [Quality Gates](/quality-gates/overview) and reported as part of [Governance](/governance/overview).
