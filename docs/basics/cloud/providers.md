## Choosing a Cloud Provider

When selecting a cloud provider for your organization or project, consider these four key criteria:

1. **Service Ecosystem & Maturity**: Ensure the provider offers robust managed services for your stack (e.g. AWS EKS for Kubernetes, GCP BigQuery for data warehousing).
2. **Global Footprint & Latency**: Choose providers with data center regions close to your primary target user base.
3. **Compliance & Governance**: Verify that the provider satisfies regulatory requirements (HIPAA, SOC 2, GDPR, ISO 27001).
4. **Cost Structure & Discounts**: Evaluate pay-as-you-go pricing, reserved instance discounts, and committed use savings.
