# Role Path: Architect

**Level:** 🟡 Intermediate - assumes basic technical/business context

## Why you need this playbook

Your job starts before the technology conversation, not with it. You're the person most responsible for making sure a solution matches a problem - not the team's favorite stack, not the most interesting technology, not the loudest opinion in the room. This path is the deepest of all the role paths because it draws on nearly every section of the playbook.

## Reading list

1. [Business & Solution Foundations Overview](/business-foundations/overview) - read the whole section, not just the highlights; this is the philosophy your architecture decisions need to trace back to.
2. [Choosing the Right Solution](/business-foundations/choosing-the-right-solution) - the decision framework you'll apply and defend on every engagement.
3. [Application vs Automation](/business-foundations/application-vs-automation), [Application vs Integration](/business-foundations/application-vs-integration), [Application vs Data Platform](/business-foundations/application-vs-data-platform) - the three comparisons you'll be asked to arbitrate most often.
4. [Automation & Integration Overview](/automation-integration/overview) - the deeper technical patterns once automation or integration is the right call.
5. [Data & Analytics Overview](/data-analytics/overview) - the deeper technical patterns once a data platform is genuinely justified, and the signals for when it isn't.
6. [Architecture Standards](/architecture/standards) - monorepo/polyrepo, event-driven patterns, and the microservice checklist.
7. [API Standards](/architecture/api-standards) - REST/GraphQL conventions, error schemas, idempotency.
8. [Solution Design](/delivery-lifecycle/design-phase) - where your architecture decisions get formalized before build starts.
9. [Governance Overview](/governance/overview) and [Compliance Model](/governance/compliance-model) - the approval and compliance context your designs operate inside.
10. [Case Studies Overview](/case-studies/overview) - real engagements, written up as short Challenge / Solution / Outcome summaries. See [Choosing the Right Solution](/business-foundations/choosing-the-right-solution) for the decision framework to apply when weighing alternatives on your own designs.
11. [Measuring Business Value](/business-foundations/measuring-business-value) - how to build the business case into the architecture decision itself, not bolt it on afterward.
12. [Disaster Recovery & Backups](/operations/disaster-recovery) and [Observability](/operations/observability) - the operational realities your architecture has to survive.

## You'll know you're ready when you can answer

::: info Ready check
- Can you justify, in terms a client would accept, why this problem needs an application and not a data platform (or vice versa)?
- What alternatives did you explicitly consider and reject, and why - could you write that down the way the case studies do?
- What are the cost, scalability, reliability, and operational trade-offs of your recommended architecture, and are they proportionate to the problem?
- If this system needs to scale 10x, what breaks first, and is that an acceptable risk today?
:::
