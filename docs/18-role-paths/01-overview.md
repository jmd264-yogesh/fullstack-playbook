# Role-Based Learning Paths

**Level:** 🟢 Beginner - readable with no technical background

This playbook is one body of content used by very different people - a client who has never written code, a fresher on day one, an architect justifying a six-month build, a QA engineer writing test plans. Nobody should have to read the whole thing cover to cover to find what matters to them.

This section adds **no new content**. Every link below points to a page that already exists elsewhere in the playbook. What this section provides is a curated, ordered reading path for each role - the sequence that role should actually read things in, with a short reason for each stop.

## How to use a role path

Each page has three parts:

1. **Why this role needs this playbook** - a short paragraph on what this role is expected to get out of it.
2. **An ordered reading list** - read top to bottom; each link has a one-line reason.
3. **A readiness checklist** - a handful of questions you should be able to answer once you've worked through the path. If you can't answer them, that's a sign to slow down on the relevant page rather than skip ahead.

If your role spans more than one of these (e.g. a tech lead who also does hands-on QA review), read both paths - the overlap is intentional, since the same page often matters for different reasons depending on who's reading it.
