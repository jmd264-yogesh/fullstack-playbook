# Role Path: Product / Business Analyst / Delivery / Project Manager

**Level:** 🟡 Intermediate - assumes basic technical/business context

## Why you need this playbook

You sit between the business problem and the engineering team, which means you need enough of both languages to translate accurately in both directions - turning a business need into a requirement engineers can act on, and turning delivery status into something a stakeholder can actually use to make decisions.

## Reading list

1. [What is a Business Problem?](/business-foundations/what-is-a-business-problem) - the discipline of getting to the real problem before requirements are written, which is squarely your responsibility.
2. [Understanding Business Processes](/business-foundations/understanding-business-processes) - how to map a process properly, including the exceptions people don't mention unprompted.
3. [From Manual to Digital](/business-foundations/manual-to-digital) and [Choosing the Right Solution](/business-foundations/choosing-the-right-solution) - so you can sanity-check a proposed solution against the actual problem before it goes to engineering.
4. [Measuring Business Value](/business-foundations/measuring-business-value) - capturing the baseline and defining "done" in business terms, ideally before delivery starts.
5. [Delivery Lifecycle Overview](/delivery-lifecycle/overview) - the full phase-by-phase path work takes here.
6. [Requirement Intake](/delivery-lifecycle/requirement-intake) - the different states a requirement can arrive in, and how each is handled.
7. [Agile Board Standards](/delivery-lifecycle/agile-board-standards) - how work is tracked, sized, and reported on here.
8. [Solution Design](/delivery-lifecycle/design-phase) - your role in reviewing and signing off on a design before build starts.
9. [Monitoring & Hypercare](/delivery-lifecycle/monitoring-phase) - what happens right after go-live, and what you should be watching.
10. [Governance Overview](/governance/overview) and [Approvals](/governance/approvals) - the sign-off points in the process.
11. [Delivery Performance](/kpis/delivery-performance) and [Engineering Metrics](/kpis/engineering-metrics) - the numbers you'll report on and be asked about.
12. [Case Studies Overview](/case-studies/overview) - full examples of the problem-to-outcome arc you're responsible for managing.

## You'll know you're ready when you can answer

::: info Ready check
- Have you captured a measurable baseline for this problem before delivery starts, so you can prove impact afterward?
- Can you explain to the engineering team why a request matters in business terms, not just what was asked for?
- What phase is this piece of work in right now, and what has to happen for it to move to the next one?
- Who has to approve this before it ships, and has that approval actually been obtained?
:::
