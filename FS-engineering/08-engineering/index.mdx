## Engineering

### What is Engineering, in this playbook?

**Engineering** covers the day-to-day practices that keep a full-stack team moving fast without breaking things - how branches move through git, how environments are managed, what makes the developer experience good or bad, and how the team documents what it builds.

In simple terms: **engineering answers the question, "How do we work together on this codebase, every day?"**

### Who is this for?

- **Engineers** looking for the team's branching model and environment conventions
- **Tech Leads** improving developer experience on a project
- **New joiners** ramping up on how a project is actually run day to day

### How to navigate

This chapter has one episode - **Practices** - covering git branching, environments, developer experience, and documentation.
