## Templates

### What are these templates?

This chapter collects the reusable, fill-in-the-blanks documents used across engagements - a **project kickoff checklist** for starting an engagement on the right foot, and a **production readiness checklist** for gating a go-live decision.

In simple terms: **templates answer the question, "What checklist should I be filling out right now?"**

### Who is this for?

- **Delivery Leads** running project kickoffs
- **Tech Leads** signing off on production readiness before go-live

### How to navigate

This chapter has one episode - **Templates** - covering project kickoff and production readiness.
