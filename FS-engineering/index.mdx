## FS Engineering Playbook

The FS Engineering Playbook is JMAN's living reference for how we design, build, test, and operate full-stack applications. It codifies the patterns, standards, and lessons learned across engagements - so every project starts from a proven foundation rather than a blank page.

### Who is this for?

- **Full-Stack Engineers** building and maintaining frontend and backend applications
- **Architects** designing application architecture for clients
- **Delivery Leads** scoping work and aligning teams on standards

### How to navigate

The playbook is organised into **Chapters**, **Episodes**, and **Pages**. Each chapter covers a major area of full-stack delivery. Episodes group related topics within a chapter. Pages are individual deep-dives you can read independently.
