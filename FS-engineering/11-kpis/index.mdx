## KPIs

### What are these KPIs?

This chapter defines the metrics used to measure full-stack delivery across three dimensions: **delivery performance** (how fast and predictably we ship), **engineering metrics** (the health of the codebase and pipeline), and **quality metrics** (defect rates, coverage, and production stability).

In simple terms: **KPIs answer the question, "How do we know if delivery is actually going well?"**

### Who is this for?

- **Delivery Leads** reporting on project health
- **Engineering Managers** benchmarking teams across engagements
- **Architects and Tech Leads** identifying where a project is trending off track
