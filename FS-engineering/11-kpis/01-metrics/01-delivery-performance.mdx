## Delivery & Productivity Metrics

While DORA measures operational health, we need to measure how effectively the team delivers business value. We use metrics aligned with the **SPACE Framework** (Satisfaction, Performance, Activity, Communication, Efficiency).

## 1. Sprint Predictability
- **Definition**: (Completed Story Points / Committed Story Points) * 100.
- **Target**: 85% - 100%.
- **Analysis**: 
  - *Consistently < 85%*: The team is over-committing, suffering from scope creep, or facing hidden technical debt.
  - *Consistently > 100%*: The team is under-committing or sandbagging.

## 2. Cycle Time (Lead Time for Work)
- **Definition**: The time elapsed from when a Jira ticket moves to "In Progress" until it is moved to "Done".
- **Target**: Less than 3 days per User Story.
- **Analysis**: Long cycle times usually highlight bottlenecks in the PR review phase, QA bottlenecks, or User Stories that were not broken down sufficiently (Violating the 'Small' INVEST criteria).

## 3. PR Review Time
- **Definition**: Time from when a PR is opened to when it is merged.
- **Target**: Less than 24 hours.
- **Analysis**: Fast PR reviews are the hallmark of a healthy engineering culture. Long PR times lead to merge conflicts and context switching.

## Metric Anti-Patterns (What we do NOT measure)
- ❌ **Lines of Code (LOC) written**: Incentivizes bloated, over-engineered code.
- ❌ **Number of Commits**: Incentivizes artificial commit padding.
- ❌ **Comparing Velocity between teams**: Story points are relative to the specific team. Team A's "5 points" is not equivalent to Team B's "5 points". Velocity is strictly for internal capacity planning.
