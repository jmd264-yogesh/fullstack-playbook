## Platform Selection Calculator

<Callout type="info">
  **Quantified Recommendations** - This calculator applies the JMAN scoring methodology to produce defensible platform recommendations. Complete each section in order for accurate results.
</Callout>

### How to Use

1. **Set Component Weights** - Adjust importance based on client workload priorities
2. **Assess Client Fit** - Score the client on each dimension
3. **Review Results** - See calculated scores and recommendation

The calculator applies:
- Component capability scores (pre-assessed by JMAN)
- Client-adjusted weightings
- Vendor capability modifiers
- Client fit modifiers

### Interpreting Results

#### Score Ranges

| Range | Interpretation |
|-------|----------------|
| **4.5+** | Strong recommendation; clear platform leader |
| **4.0-4.5** | Good fit; viable option |
| **3.5-4.0** | Adequate but with trade-offs |
| **Below 3.5** | Significant concerns; investigate alternatives |

#### Score Difference

| Difference | Interpretation |
|------------|----------------|
| **>0.5** | Clear winner; high confidence |
| **0.2-0.5** | Likely winner; moderate confidence |
| **&lt;0.2** | Close call; consider other factors |

#### Client Fit Indicators

| Indicator | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|
| **Strong Fit** | Client profile closely matches platform expectations |
| **Good Fit** | Minor misalignments, manageable |
| **Neutral** | Neither strong alignment nor misalignment |
| **Weak Fit** | Significant misalignments; delivery risk |
| **Poor Fit** | Major misalignments; reconsider platform |
