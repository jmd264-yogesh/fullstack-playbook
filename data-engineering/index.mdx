## Data Engineering Playbook

The Data Engineering Playbook is JMAN's living reference for how we design, build, and operate modern data platforms. It codifies the patterns, standards, and lessons learned across engagements — so every project starts from a proven foundation rather than a blank page.

### Who is this for?

- **Data Engineers** building and maintaining pipelines, modelling data for business consumption
- **Architects** designing platform architecture for clients
- **Delivery Leads** scoping work and aligning teams on standards

### How to navigate

The playbook is organised into **Chapters**, **Episodes**, and **Pages**. Each chapter covers a major area of data engineering. Episodes group related topics within a chapter. Pages are individual deep-dives you can read independently.
